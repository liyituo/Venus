# Limitations

- This project is for research and engineering demonstration only.
- It is not investment advice and makes no claim that a strategy can profit.
- Backtests, fixtures, and simulated fills do not represent live trading.
- No jurisdiction-specific legal, regulatory, suitability, or compliance assessment has been completed.
- The project does not connect to real market data, real brokers, real funds, credentials, notifications, or operating-system schedules.
- `LiveBroker` is intentionally disabled and rejects every call.
- The demo data is synthetic and local; it is not a market-data entitlement or production feed.
- SQLite/JSONL storage is suitable for a local MVP, not a multi-user production ledger.
- Authentication and authorization in the future integration contract are placeholders.
- Concurrency, distributed locks, settlement, corporate actions, tax lots, shorting, margin, and complex order types are not implemented.
- Production use would require independent security, risk, compliance, operational, and broker-adapter review.
- Real-money trading must remain disabled until compliance, risk, and controlled small-scale validation are complete.
